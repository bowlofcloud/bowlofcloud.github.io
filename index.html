Selected projects

AI research writing pipeline

Team project for my MSc, built in Python with the OpenAI API and Streamlit

AI-drafted papers are fast to produce but easy to spot, and their claims can drift from the sources.

I built the detector that scores how AI-written a paragraph sounds, and a pipeline where one model rewrites each paragraph and a second checks it for facts and meaning before it is kept.

Singapore haze forecast

Python, scikit-learn and Streamlit, with NEA, weather and NASA satellite data

Haze can push Singapore’s air into the unhealthy range within a day, so warning even a few days ahead matters.

I built a pipeline that combines satellite fire detections over Sumatra and Borneo with air-quality and weather data, forecasts each region’s PSI up to three days ahead, and refreshes itself every six hours.

Expressway image filter

Python, PyTorch, CLIP and scikit-learn

The task: keep only Singapore expressway photos from about 1,000 noisy road images, with as little manual work and computing cost as possible.

I used a pre-trained image model and labelled only the photos it was unsure about, then traced most remaining errors to road markings lost at low resolution.

  • 0.875F1 score on held-out images
  • 34 minof manual labelling in total
  • 56/simages processed on a laptop, with no API cost
